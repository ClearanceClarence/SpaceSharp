# SpaceSharp is installed

You'll find SpaceSharp in the Start menu.

**Getting started**

1. Pick a drive and click **Scan drive**, or click **Scan folder** to look at one folder.
2. The biggest boxes are the biggest space users. Hover for details, double-click to zoom in.
3. Right-click a box to open it, show it in Explorer, or move it to the Recycle Bin.

Press **F1** in the app for the full list of shortcuts, and the gear button opens the settings.
